# Anti-slop (adapted)

mblode word lists and AI patterns. Distilled. Not a vendor. Any site category.

A phrase already in the project's brief or in its live UI outranks a list word.

## Never write

exclamation marks, seamless, effortless, magic, powerful, simply, just, easily,
unlock, supercharge, revolutionary, game-changing, streamline, empower,
world-class, enterprise-grade, delve, leverage (verb), robust, holistic,
paradigm, cutting-edge, synergy, showcase, tapestry, realm, embark, beacon,
utilise, nestled, vibrant, deep dive, unpack, intricate, actionable, impactful,
learnings, thought leadership, best practices, in order to, due to the fact that.

Also: "simple" as an unearned claim ("our simple onboarding").

## Flag when 2+ in one paragraph

harness, navigate, foster, elevate, unleash, bolster, spearhead, resonate,
revolutionise, facilitate, nuanced, crucial, ecosystem (metaphor), myriad,
plethora, catalyse, transformative, cornerstone, paramount, burgeoning, nascent.

## Patterns (fix before ship)

- Em dash (`—`), `--`, or spaced hyphen ` - ` as a dash. Comma, colon, period,
  or parentheses.
- `Not X. A Y.` repeated down the page. One hero thesis is allowed. Adjective
  triads (`Secure, Safe, and Trustworthy`) are not a thesis.
- Theater framing. Say what it does.
- Same label repeated inside one container.
- How it works, Features, Our story, Get in touch, Why choose us as an h2
  or kicker.
- Kicker / eyebrow / hero pill above the heading.
- Prompt echo: handing the brief's wording back.
- Process slogan: how the thing is built, as a headline.
- Invented local color: a town, trade, or customer the brief did not supply.
- Generic default over a supplied specific.
- Chatbot artefacts: "I hope this helps", "Great question", "Let's dive in."
- "Whether you're X or Y", "in today's [x]", "here's the thing", "your [x]
  journey", "serves as" / "boasts" instead of "is".
- Vague attributions: "experts believe", "studies show" with no named source.
- List headline: `Noun, noun, and noun` as an h1/h2.
- Instruction hero: two steps as a slogan (`Do X, then click Y`).
- UI narration as the hero: a tour of the website instead of the business.
- Empty epithet: The {category} for {generic audience}.
- Tautology heading: the opposite would be a bug.
- Empty process: continuity or layout as the heading.
- Free as the heading: selling the free tier from the section title.
  A price on a plan card is fine.

## Swap test

On body, proof, **and** an empty hero epithet: if a competitor in this
category can paste their name on the line unchanged, it fails. Do not use
the swap test to demand a number on a named role.

## `[VERIFY]`

Unproven claim in a draft: mark `[VERIFY]`. Do not invent a number to clear
the mark. Shipped copy: resolve (supplied fact) or delete the claim. Never
leave the token in UI or HTML.
